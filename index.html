How I work

Calm beats chaotic.

The point is not to look busy. The point is to isolate the issue, make the smallest safe change, test it, and explain the fix in a way you can actually reuse.

1

Clarify the goal

We define what “fixed” means, what changed recently, and who is affected.

2

Baseline before changing

I check the current state first, so we are not guessing blindly in production.

3

Apply the smallest safe fix

One controlled change at a time, with rollback thinking when the system matters.

4

Verify and document

You get a short handover: what was wrong, what changed, and how to validate it later.
